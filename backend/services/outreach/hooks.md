# Video hooks

The opening line of each outreach video script comes from this library.
Each `###` section below is one hook style: a line on how it works, then hooks to adapt.
`pitch.py` gives every lead one style, chosen from its lead ID so the same club always gets the same style and the 110-odd scripts do not all open the same way.
Only the chosen section goes into the prompt; this preamble never does.

The styles are the patterns that keep showing up in 2026's viral Reels and TikTok hook lists (identity calls, open loops, POVs, questions, results first, hot takes, founder confessions), rewritten for a 30 to 60 second video Tony sends a club's exec team by email.
Sources: creatorsjet.com/blog/instagram-reel-hooks, teleprompter.com/blog/hooks-for-instagram-reels, socialync.io/viral-hooks-library, virlo.ai/data/viral-hooks, taggbox.com/blog/best-instagram-hooks.

What every hook here has to be, and what to check before adding one:
- True: it claims nothing about the club we cannot see in its events, and nothing about Wat2Do the prompt does not say.
- Kind: it never suggests the club's events flop, and never knocks another club.
- Paid off: the rest of the video answers whatever the hook opens.
- Safe for a club inbox: no shock bait, fake urgency, money flexing, looks, dating, politics or religion.
- No "stop scrolling" or "don't scroll": the video arrives by email, not in a feed.
- `<club>` and `<event>` stand for the club's name and one of its real events (its upcoming one, when it has one), which Claude fills in; `{{school_name}}` and `{{payout_per_scan}}` (the club payout per scanned student, such as "$2") are filled in like the prompt's own, and no other `{{` key works here.

### Identity call

Name the people watching, so they know in one second this video is theirs and not a mass email.
- "If you're on the <club> exec team, give me thirty seconds."
- "This one goes out to whoever planned <event>."
- "<club> execs, quick one."
- "If you've ever made the poster, the post and the story for <event> all by yourself, this is for you."
- "To whoever runs the <club> Instagram: I've got something for you."
- "If you're the one buying snacks for <event>, listen to this part."

### Open loop

Start a thought they need to hear the end of, then finish it before the video ends.
- "I found <club> because of <event>, and honestly, that's the problem."
- "So I already made <club> something. You just haven't seen it yet."
- "We already caught <event>. It's just missing one thing: you."
- "There's a reason the same faces show up to every club event."
- "Okay, I've got kind of a weird offer for <club>."
- "Here's the part of running a club nobody tells you about."
- "I was looking through <event> and had one thought."

### POV

Drop them into a moment every club exec knows.
Keep it a scene they would picture, never a claim about what happened at their events.
- "POV: <event> is packed and you don't recognize half the room."
- "POV: someone at <event> tells you they found it on Wat2Do."
- "POV: it's ten minutes before <event> and you're doing a headcount in your head."
- "POV: you're the exec making the poster, the post, the story and the reminder for <event>."
- "POV: your next event pays for its own snacks."
- "POV: you're scanning people into <event>, and every scan pays the club {{payout_per_scan}}."
- "POV: the door at <event> is just you, your phone's camera and a line that keeps moving."

### Question

Ask something they will answer in their head before you finish the sentence.
- "Real question: how do people find out about <event> right now?"
- "How many people at <event> had never heard of <club> before?"
- "What would <event> look like with twice the people?"
- "Be honest, how much of your budget goes to snacks?"
- "Would you take {{payout_per_scan}} for every student you scan into <event>?"
- "Did you know <event> is already on Wat2Do?"

### Results first

Lead with the most concrete thing they get, then explain it.
- "{{payout_per_scan}} for every student you scan into your next event."
- "<event> is already on Wat2Do. Claim <club>, and it gets a verified check and free QR tickets."
- "<club> already has a page on Wat2Do. You just haven't claimed it."
- "Your <club> page is already up, and <event> is on it."
- "Your next <event> could pay for part of itself."
- "One tap, and every <club> event on Wat2Do gets a verified check."

### Hot take

A true, mildly spicy take about running a club, one they would nod at.
- "Hot take: planning <event> is the easy part. Getting people there is the hard part."
- "Nobody joins a club for the exec meetings. They join for nights like <event>."
- "Unpopular opinion: <event> deserves more people than an Instagram story can reach."
- "The best events on campus aren't the hard ones to run. They're the hard ones to find."
- "Every club has that one event people talk about after. For <club>, it's <event>."

### Founder confession

One honest line about Tony, then turn it to them fast.
Use only what the prompt says about him: a software engineer on co-op at Amazon who builds Wat2Do on the side, because good club events should not go unnoticed.
- "I'm on co-op at Amazon, and outside of work I'm building one thing: Wat2Do."
- "I'm a software engineer at Amazon, and I've been spending every spare hour on this."
- "Wat2Do is my side project, and <club> is the kind of club I built it for."
- "I work at Amazon on co-op, and the thing I can't stop thinking about is events like <event>."
- "I build software at Amazon by day, and the problem I'm trying to fix is good events nobody hears about."
