  Pick the closest common `font-family` stack for its lettering (for example `Impact,'Arial Narrow Bold',sans-serif` for condensed, `'Arial Black',sans-serif` for heavy, `Didot,'Bodoni 72',Georgia,serif` for high-contrast serif, `'Snell Roundhand','Brush Script MT',cursive` for script, `'Bradley Hand','Marker Felt',cursive` for brush), and give every `<text>` a `textLength` within the view box and `lengthAdjust="spacingAndGlyphs"` so no line is cut off whatever font a reader has.
  Give every `<text>` its own `font-size`, the size at which its words in that font are about as wide as its `textLength`: `textLength` only evens out readers' fonts, so it must not stretch or squeeze the letters (a short word gets a bigger size, a long line a smaller one).
  Keep every letter inside the view box: each baseline at least a capital's height (about 0.8 of its font size) below the top and below the line above it, descenders and outlines clear of the bottom, and H as tall as the lines need.
  Centre the title: give every `<text>` `text-anchor="middle"` at x 200, since the card centres it.
  Spell the event's title in full, every word of it, including a word the poster sets as a logo (a sponsor such as KPMG, a club's wordmark); the card shows the drawing in place of the title, so a missing word shows a wrong title.
  Letters dark enough to vanish on the fade get a light outline (a dark shadow under light letters needs none); leave out everything that is not the title's words (icons, stars, dates, times, places, and logos that are not part of the title). No scripts, images, links, styles or event handlers.
  Always draw it when there is a poster, since a featured card shows the drawing: when the poster's lettering is not this event's title (a header such as WEEK AHEAD or SCHEDULE, or a series name without the event), or its title is plain or not shown, set the event's title yourself in the poster's main colours and the font closest to its lettering.
  Use NULL only when there is no poster.
